# Reqistry Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026. Canonical version: https://reqistry.dev/privacy

Reqistry is an API discovery catalog for developers. This policy covers the Reqistry Android app, website, and API.

**Information We Collect**

- Account: an anonymous identifier is created on first launch; your email only if you choose to link it. Auth tokens are stored encrypted on-device (Android Keystore).
- App data: saved favorites, watchlist, settings, and notification preferences.
- Push notifications: your Firebase (FCM) token, only to deliver notifications you opt into.
- Diagnostics: crash and performance reports used to fix issues and improve the app.

**Mobile Advertising**

The Android app is free and ad-supported through Google AdMob: one standard banner on category API lists, plus clearly labeled native ads inside category and search lists. Ads never gate features. Reqistry offers no subscriptions and collects no purchase data.

Before any ad request, the app collects your consent choices through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where regulations require it, revisit those choices anytime via Settings → Privacy choices. Ad delivery and measurement are handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy); we do not receive your advertising ID.

**Data Sharing**

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by Google (Firebase push/crash reporting, AdMob ads) to operate the app, and shared with law enforcement only when required by law.

**Security**

We use encrypted on-device token storage and HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

**Your Rights**

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time, including account deletion.

**Links to Other Sites**

Reqistry links to third-party API sites and documentation. Those external sites are not operated by us; review their privacy policies. We assume no responsibility for their content, policies, or practices.

**Changes to This Privacy Policy**

We may update this policy; the current version is always at https://reqistry.dev/privacy with the updated date above.
