# 4-Week Marketing Plan for API Scout

This is a marketing schedule designed to create initial buzz and build a sustainable growth channel for your app.

---

### **Week 1: The Launch**
**Goal:** Generate a strong initial wave of traffic and downloads.

*   **Monday (Day 1): Preparation**
    *   **Task:** Finalize all marketing materials.
    *   **To-Do:**
        *   Triple-check the `marketing_copy.md` file and fill in your app's Google Play link.
        *   Prepare high-quality screenshots and maybe a short screen recording of the app in use.
        *   Create your Product Hunt account and prepare your submission (Title, Tagline, Description, Images). **Do not post it yet.**

*   **Tuesday (Day 2): LAUNCH DAY**
    *   **Task:** Post on Product Hunt and announce it to the world.
    *   **To-Do:**
        *   **Morning (around 12:01 AM PST):** Submit your app to Product Hunt.
        *   **Morning:** Announce the launch on your personal Twitter/X and LinkedIn profiles. Ask your network for support. Use the copy from `marketing_copy.md`.
        *   **Afternoon:** Post in `r/androidapps` on Reddit. Frame it as "I made this app to..."
        *   **All Day:** Monitor Product Hunt, Twitter, and Reddit. Respond to EVERY comment. This is crucial.

*   **Wednesday (Day 3): Community Engagement**
    *   **Task:** Share in more communities.
    *   **To-Do:**
        *   Post in `r/sideproject`, `r/flutterdev`, and `r/programming`. Tailor the message slightly for each community.
        *   Engage with any new comments on yesterday's posts.

*   **Thursday (Day 4): Content Creation**
    *   **Task:** Write and publish your first blog post.
    *   **To-Do:**
        *   Write an article on **Dev.to** or **Hashnode**.
        *   **Title Idea:** "I Launched My First App on Product Hunt - Here's What Happened." (People love behind-the-scenes stories).
        *   Include a link to your app.

*   **Friday (Day 5): Content Promotion**
    *   **Task:** Share your blog post.
    *   **To-Do:**
        *   Share the link to your article on Twitter/X and LinkedIn.
        *   Post it to relevant communities if their rules allow it (some subreddits have a "Showdev" or "Shameless Plug" thread).

---

### **Week 2: Build on Momentum**
**Goal:** Create more content and start building a social media presence.

*   **Monday (Day 8): New Content Angle**
    *   **Task:** Write a second blog post.
    *   **To-Do:**
        *   Publish an article on a different platform (e.g., if you used Dev.to last week, use Medium this week).
        *   **Title Idea:** "How I Built a Flutter App to Browse 1400+ Public APIs." Talk about your development journey.

*   **Wednesday (Day 10): Social Proof**
    *   **Task:** Share a win.
    *   **To-Do:**
        *   Post a "milestone" update on Twitter/X and LinkedIn. Did you hit 100 downloads? 500? Get a great review? Share it!
        *   "Wow! Blown away by the support for my API Scout app. Just passed 500 downloads in the first week. Thank you all!"

*   **Friday (Day 12): Feature Highlight**
    *   **Task:** Showcase the app's value.
    *   **To-Do:**
        *   Create a short GIF or screen recording of a specific feature (like the search or bookmarking).
        *   Post it on Twitter with a caption like: "Did you know you can bookmark your favorite APIs in my app? Makes it super easy to come back to them later. #devTools #AndroidDev"

---

### **Week 3: Expand Your Reach**
**Goal:** Find new communities and start direct outreach.

*   **Monday (Day 15): Explore New Platforms**
    *   **Task:** Find and post in niche communities.
    *   **To-Do:**
        *   Search for Facebook groups like "Flutter Developers," "SaaS Founders," or "Indie Hackers." Read the rules, and if allowed, share your app.
        *   Look for relevant Discord servers.

*   **Wednesday (Day 17): Direct Outreach**
    *   **Task:** Contact newsletters or content creators.
    *   **To-Do:**
        *   Find 3-5 tech newsletters or YouTubers who feature developer tools or new apps.
        *   Send them a short, personalized email. "Hi [Name], I'm a big fan of your [newsletter/channel]. I just launched a free app for developers and thought your audience might find it useful. [Link to app]."

*   **Friday (Day 19): User Feedback**
    *   **Task:** Publicly ask for feedback.
    *   **To-Do:**
        *   Post on Twitter/X: "What's one feature you'd love to see in the API Scout app? I'm planning the next update and would love your ideas."

---

### **Week 4 & Beyond: Long-Term Growth**
**Goal:** Analyze results and establish an ongoing marketing habit.

*   **Monday (Day 22): Analyze and Learn**
    *   **Task:** Review your efforts.
    *   **To-Do:**
        *   Check your Google Play Console analytics. Where did your downloads come from?
        *   Which posts got the most engagement? Double down on what works.

*   **Wednesday (Day 24): Share Your Journey**
    *   **Task:** Write a retrospective blog post.
    *   **To-Do:**
        *   Publish an article: "One Month After Launch: 10 Things I Learned Marketing My Indie App."
        *   This kind of transparent content is very valuable to other developers and can attract a lot of attention.

*   **Ongoing Tasks:**
    *   **Weekly:** Share one interesting API you found using your own app on social media.
    *   **Bi-Weekly:** Post updates on your app's progress (even small bug fixes).
    *   **Monthly:** Engage in relevant online communities. Don't just promote—answer questions and be helpful.

This plan should give you a great start. Remember to be genuine and passionate about your project, and people will notice.
